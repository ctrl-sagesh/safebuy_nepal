How We Verify

What we check before a seller is trusted

Full transparency: here are the exact criteria behind every trust score, what each level means, and how a seller earns verification.

🪪

Identity verified

National ID uploaded and manually approved by an admin — no bots, no auto-approval.

📱

Contact confirmed

Phone number (OTP) and email verified so a real, reachable person is behind the account.

🏢

Business legitimacy

PAN / business registration (optional) boosts trust and signals an official, accountable seller.

📉

Fraud record

Severity-weighted fraud reports. Serious, repeated, evidence-backed reports lower the score most.

⭐

Authentic reviews

Positive & negative reviews — each passes six fake-review checks so nobody can game the rating.

⏳

Account age & disputes

Older accounts and fairly-resolved disputes earn more trust than brand-new pages.

80–100🟢 Trusted

Verified identity and a clean record. Reasonably safe — still prefer Cash on Delivery when you can.

50–79🟠 Unverified

Limited data or minor flags. Proceed with caution, avoid large advance payments, ask for COD.

0–49🔴 High Risk

Multiple or serious reports. Do not pay in advance. May be escalated to the Cyber Bureau.

Non-Delivery

Seller moves the chat to WhatsApp/Viber, takes advance payment via QR, then blocks you and disappears. Most common on TikTok and Instagram.

28%

Fake Products

Counterfeit items sold as branded originals, especially cosmetics and electronics.

20%

Payment Fraud

eSewa/Khalti reversals, QR code swaps, and fake payment confirmations.

12%

Account Cloning

Fake pages impersonating legitimate businesses to steal customer payments.
