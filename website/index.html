Common Scam Types in Nepal

35%

Non-Delivery

Seller moves the chat to WhatsApp/Viber, takes advance payment via QR, then blocks you and disappears. Most common on TikTok and Instagram.

28%

Fake Products

Counterfeit items sold as branded originals, especially cosmetics and electronics.

20%

Payment Fraud

eSewa/Khalti reversals, QR code swaps, and fake payment confirmations.

12%

Account Cloning

Fake pages impersonating legitimate businesses to steal customer payments.
